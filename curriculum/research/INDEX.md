# Pedagogical research — the METHOD layer

**These five reports are the reason an enriched lesson is more than a re-typed textbook page.** The
textbook supplies the *content spine*; these supply the *method* — the evidence-based elements a
government textbook systematically omits. Stage C (enrichment) is required to inject them, and the
scoring gate (`../gates/`) scores for them — **research wins on any clash with the imported rubric**.

All are decision-grade, with citations inline and a full source list per file.

## How to reuse these in a new market

The **mechanisms** are universal; the **norms and the language pair are not**. Port as follows:

| Portable as-is | Must be re-derived per market |
|---|---|
| Simple View, Scarborough, orthographic mapping, systematic phonics, decode+encode reciprocity | The **L1↔L2 pair** and therefore which phonemes need explicit articulation work (Urdu→English gives th, v/w, short vowels; a different L1 gives a different list) |
| CPA order, number sense, retrieval + spacing, variation, mastery gate | Fluency **norms** (WCPM/cwpm) and the national assessment ladder (ASER here; EGRA/other elsewhere) |
| 5E + POE, investigate-before-explain | The **script's** decoding demands (Nastaliq positional forms + dots + aeraab is Urdu-specific; Arabic shares much of it, Kiswahili/Latin almost none) |
| BICS/CALP, translanguaging policy per phase | Which language is L1 vs medium-of-instruction, and the official language policy for maths/science |
| The textbook-gap tables (what a typical book omits) | Verify against **this** market's actual book — the gap list is an expectation, not a finding |

**Do not import another market's targets as if they were measured here.** The ICT numbers below the
research (WCPM, cwpm, ASER rungs, automaticity ages) are design targets anchored to Pakistani
evidence. A new market re-derives them from its own national assessment before the enrichment brief
quotes them.
